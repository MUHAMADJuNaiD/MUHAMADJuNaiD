## Want more professional tracks? Download them yourself (free, commercial use)

These sites are blocked from the cloud machine, so download them on your PC and drop the MP3s into `music/`:

- **Pixabay Music** (pixabay.com/music): no attribution needed. Search for: `fashion`, `fashion house 120 bpm`, `luxury`, `glamour`, `upbeat pop`, `deep house`, `chic`, `runway`.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (studio.youtube.com, then Audio Library): filter **"Attribution not required"**, genre *Dance & Electronic* or *Pop*, mood *Bright* or *Glamorous*.
- Pick tracks around **120 BPM** so the cuts land on the beat.
- **Never** use Bollywood or other famous copyrighted songs inside the video, or Instagram may mute or block it. Add trending audio inside the Instagram app instead.
