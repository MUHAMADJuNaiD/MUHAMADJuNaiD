## Sound effects (`sfx/`)

Every base sound has 2–4 versions (`name--v1.wav` …), plus story sounds:

- `camera-shutter`: Runway flashes
- `paper-rustle`: Unbox
- `wind`: winter Season reels
- `bell-hit`, `pluck-up`, `sparkle-sweep`, `snap`, `bass-hit`: extra choices for reveal, colour and button moments

Each reel picks its own mix:
- every cue slot has alternatives, and the version is chosen per product
- the same sound is never used twice in one reel, except the runway camera flashes
- sound effects stay quiet under the music

To regenerate the pack: `python3 make_pack.py`. To add your own royalty-free music: drop the MP3 into `music/` and set `"music": "file.mp3"` on a reel.
