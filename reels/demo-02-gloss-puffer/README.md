# Reel demo 02: "Gloss Puffer" (Hero Product template)

- `outlook-styles-reel-gloss-puffer-2k.mp4`: 16 s, **1440 × 2560 (2K)**, 30 fps, H.264 High (CRF 12), no audio
- `cover.jpg`: cover / thumbnail
- `safe-zone-check.jpg`: frames with Instagram UI zones marked in red, showing all content stays in the clear area

## Changes after feedback on demo 01

1. **One theme per reel.** One product and one colour world, lime and violet like the approved poster. The brand sting, 3D word, podium, button and callouts all use the same palette, and nothing is mixed.
2. **Only heavy-quality products.** The gloss puffer was chosen because its HD cut-out is crisp at 2K, with clean shine, zip and stitching. Products whose cut-out is not sharp enough at this size are skipped.
3. **Instagram safe zone.** All content stays between y 380 and y 2000 (of 2560), and clear of the right-side icons below y 1300. The camera only sways slightly (±6°, with a 6 % push-in for details), so nothing drifts in and out.
