# Outlook Styles – Demo v3: "Colour Pop" theme

Changes based on the feedback on v2:

1. **Colour combination.** The big word is no longer the same colour as the background. Each poster has a bold background and one contrasting "pop" colour. That pop colour is used for the big word, the highlighted part of the headline, and the CTA button.
2. **More prominent and attractive design.** The big word is solid and fully readable. The headline is bigger (80 px) and part of it is coloured. "DM TO ORDER" is now a filled button, and the category name sits in a pill tag.
3. **Only products with clean cut-outs.** All 357 photos were cut out and checked. 164 products are approved (see `approved-products.jpg` and the list below). Bulk production will use only these.

- `posters/` – 15 demo posters (1080 × 1350)
- `instagram-grid-preview.jpg` – profile grid with the 3:4 crop
- `all-posters-overview.jpg` – all posters on one sheet
- `approved-products.jpg` / `approved.json` – products approved for bulk
- `source/` – generator (`gen3.py`), configs (`posters3.json`), cut-out scripts and cut-outs

## Product audit: 164 approved out of 357 photos

Approved if the cut-out is clean, there is no plastic wrap, no hanging tag, no box or case in the frame, and no big third-party logo or licensed print.

Main reasons photos were skipped:
- **Bags:** most have plastic wrap, a price tag, a box, or a big brand monogram or print. Only 6 clean bags are left.
- **Sunglasses:** most are photographed with branded boxes or cases. 12 clean frames are left.
- **Clothing:** prints with big brand names or licensed characters, white stripes lost in the cut-out, low resolution, or visible tags.
